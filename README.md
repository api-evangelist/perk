Perk (formerly TravelPerk) is the intelligent platform for corporate travel and spend management, combining travel booking with expense, invoice, and card controls for finance teams. Founded in Barcelona in 2015, Perk serves thousands of global teams and is backed by General Catalyst, SoftBank Vision Fund, Speedinvest and others. Its developer platform exposes a Travel & Spend REST API, a SCIM 2.0 user-provisioning API, webhooks, a sandbox, a public Postman collection, and an official hosted Model Context Protocol (MCP) server for AI clients.

**Areas:** Company · Corporate Travel · Expense Management · Spend Management · Travel · Invoices · Fintech · Software-as-a-Service

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Perk: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
